# Version 3.0.0 changes

- Verse-by-verse Quran recitation with 31 reciters: the verse being recited is highlighted and pages turn to follow along. Play from any verse; downloaded verses replay without internet.
- Recitation keeps playing in the background, with controls in the notification, on the lock screen and on headphones. It pauses for calls and other audio.
- If a verse fails to download, recitation continues with the next verse.
- Redesigned Quran audio screen that matches the Quran reader.
- New languages: Kurdish (Sorani), Dari and Urdu, with Kurdish and Urdu Quran translations.
- New duas: answering the muezzin and the dua after the adhan, and the funeral prayer duas.
- A clearer, resizable prayer-times widget and shorter morning/evening adhkar notifications.
- The selected Quran search result is highlighted on its page.
- New ice badge for the weekly streak freeze.
- Website and Instagram links on the About screen.
- Fixed the double-back exit and made other interface improvements.
