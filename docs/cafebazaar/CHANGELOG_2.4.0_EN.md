# Version 2.4.0 changes

- Verse-by-verse Quran recitation with 31 reciters; the verse being recited is highlighted and pages turn to follow along.
- New Quran audio section with 50 reciters and offline Surah playback after the first download.
- Share Quran verses as an image or text, with or without translation.
- New languages: Dari (Afghanistan) and Urdu, with an Urdu Quran translation.
- Customizable quick access on the home screen.
- Three new adhan voices: Abdulbasit Abdusamad, Nasser Al Qatami and Yasser Al-Dosari.
- More reliable progress sync with your account.
- Shorter morning and evening adhkar notifications that show the app's own icon.
- A new ice badge for days kept by the weekly streak shield.
- View your submitted feedback with replies from the team.
- Improved "My statistics" charts and several fixes.
