# Bundled Adhan audio sources

The following files are bundled unchanged from the [Kiwifu Adhan MP3 collection](https://github.com/Kiwifu/adhan-mp3), whose README describes the collection as free for Islamic apps, prayer-time software, and personal use. Resource filenames differ from the source filenames; the audio bytes are unmodified. Source details stay in project documentation and are intentionally omitted from the compact in-app selector.

1. `adhan_makkah.mp3` — **Ali Ibn Ahmad Mala 1 - Al Haram Al Maki** (`علي بن أحمد ملا - الحرم المكي`).
   - SHA-1: `a16fb14b22397c22fb809187e81d3b3ef9a02f29`
2. `adhan_madinah.mp3` — **Adhan Al Haram Al Madani - Al Madinah 1** (`أذان الحرم المدني - المدينة المنورة`).
   - SHA-1: `1b0602ae0af66e77d65512297eccd37ecc9e1899`
3. `adhan_alafasy.mp3` — **Mishary Rashid Alafasy 1 - Kuwait** (`مشاري راشد العفاسي - الكويت`).
   - SHA-1: `f36d1d6dbcb69692e0269aa60d3c915610e986e0`
